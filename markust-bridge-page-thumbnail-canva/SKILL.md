## Quality contract

- Source of content rules: [click-through-copy.md](references/click-through-copy.md). Four distinct page appeals, ingredient-led first page, problem/solution second; useful support beyond slogans, concise fresh copy, destination-specific CTA. No four-benefit overview by default.
- Source of visual rules: [approved-quality.md](references/approved-quality.md). Preserve approved finish, navy/light theme, hierarchy, natural framing and useful information. Copy revisions preserve current styles/photo crops; new designs use readable larger supporting type with restrained bold/accent.
- Native editable text/photos; no whole-page flattened deliverable or PPTX substitute. Use the official transparent beplain logo top-left on every page, proportions/alpha intact, no white badge.
- Every portrait uses a source pose 0°–45°. Structures 01/04 may choose different suitable angles, without treating angle as effect evidence. Structure 05 needs the same anatomical region/pose/scale/light/expression. Never label two angles of one state Before/After.
- Existing concept assets retain provenance internally; do not add AI-related wording to artwork. Where a comparison could imply measured results, use readable neutral wording such as “Illustrative comparison; not actual product-use results.” No fabricated statistics, periods, clinical charts, testimonials or generated packaging.
- Photo edits use ImageGen when authorized; placement uses crop/scale only. A packshot is optional and must be supplied or authorized official artwork.
